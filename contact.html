How to Reach Hotel Classic Hills

Hotel Classic Hills is located at Bhagsu Nag, a five-minute walk from Bhagsunag Temple and a short drive from McLeod Ganj and Dharamshala bus stand.

McLeod Ganj Bus Stand~10–15 min drive
Main McLeod Ganj Market~10 min drive
Dharamshala (main town)~25–30 min drive
Kangra Airport (Gaggal / DHM)~20 km · ~45 min–1 hr drive
Pathankot Railway Station~90 km · ~3–4.5 hr drive
Chandigarh~250 km · ~7–8 hr drive
Delhi~490 km · ~10–12 hr drive

Distances and travel times are approximate and can vary with traffic, weather and road conditions on the hill roads.
