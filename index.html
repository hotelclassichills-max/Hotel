Welcome

Himalayan hospitality, kept simple

Every balcony room looks out over the valley or the hills behind McLeod Ganj. Warm wood interiors, quiet corridors and a team that knows the neighbourhood well enough to send you to the right cafe, trek or temple.

Dharamshala & McLeod Ganj

Hotel Classic Hills sits right in Bhagsu Nag, walking distance from a temple and waterfall, and a short drive from the rest of McLeod Ganj.

~13 min walk

Bhagsunag Temple & Waterfall

An ancient Shiva temple with sacred bathing pools, a short walk further up to the waterfall itself. The closest attraction to the hotel.

~3 min drive

Dalai Lama Temple Complex

The Tsuglagkhang Complex, official residence of His Holiness the Dalai Lama and the main Tibetan monastery, with a museum and circumambulation walk.

~3–5 min drive

McLeod Ganj Market

The main market and Mall Road area — Tibetan handicrafts, cafes, bookshops and street food, right in the centre of town.

~15–20 min drive

Naddi & Dal Lake

A quieter viewpoint village above McLeod Ganj, popular for sunset over the Dhauladhar range, with the small Dal Lake nearby.

~20 min drive to trailhead

Triund Trek

The region's best-known beginner trek, starting near Dharamkot or Gallu Devi Temple and climbing to sweeping Himalayan views.

~30–40 min drive

Dharamshala Cricket Stadium (HPCA)

One of India's most scenic cricket grounds, set against the Dhauladhar range, down in lower Dharamshala.

All travel times are approximate and can vary with traffic, weather and road conditions.
