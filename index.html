Welcome

Himalayan hospitality, kept simple

Tucked into the hillside at Bhagsunag, Hotel Classic Hills is a small, family-run property built around one idea — a genuinely comfortable stay with the Dhauladhar range never far from view.

Every balcony room looks out over the valley or the hills behind McLeod Ganj. Warm wood interiors, quiet corridors and a team that knows the neighbourhood well enough to send you to the right cafe, trek or temple.
